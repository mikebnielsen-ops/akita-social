# AKITA screen and clip library

Screenshots and short clips of the AKITA app for social posts and reels.

Everything here comes from the fictional demo company **AKITA Demo**: invented people (first name and initial), invented brands (Veltro Energy, Quillo Mobile, Havsund Insurance, Lumetra Broadband), invented locations and invented numbers. No real customers, people or client logos appear.

- `screens/phone-*` are 1170x2532 (390x844 at 3x).
- `screens/desktop-*` are 2880x1800 (1440x900 at 2x).
- `clips/` are MP4 files without sound, recorded at calm real-time speed. Phone clips are 1170x2532, the desktop clip is 2880x1800. A blue dot shows each tap.

Reps are shown on phone and leaders on desktop. Logged in as the demo rep (Noah R.) unless the file says CEO (Morgan C.).

- Clips are not sped up. Play them at normal speed.
