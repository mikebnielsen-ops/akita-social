# AKITA competitors to learn from

Updated 1 November 2026 after a full market scan (Nordics, Europe, UK and Ireland, USA and Canada, Australia and NZ). Used by Claude to study what works in competitors' social content. Learn patterns (hook types, formats, angles), never copy their words or material.

## Rules for using this list

- Rank competitor posts by comments, shares and likes relative to the competitor's own usual level, and by how long a post or ad has run. Never by views: many competitor view counts look paid (see PLAYBOOK.md).
- Skip rep-life creator content (a day in my life, what I earned, street humour), rep mindset and podcast clips, and trade-show posts. Keep only patterns that fit the PLAYBOOK.
- Handles were found through web search, not checked on the profile itself. Metricool confirms them when they are added as competitors.
