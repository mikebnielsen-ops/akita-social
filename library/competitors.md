# AKITA competitors to learn from

Started 1 November 2026. Used by Claude to study what works in competitors' social content. Learn patterns (hook types, formats, angles), never copy their words or material.

## Rules for using this list

- Rank competitor posts by comments, saves and how long a post or ad has been running. Never by views: many competitor view counts look paid (see PLAYBOOK.md).
- Skip rep-life creator content (a day in my life, what I earned, street humour). Mike has ruled it out for AKITA. Keep only patterns that fit the PLAYBOOK.
- Handles were found through web search, not checked on the profile itself. Metricool confirms them when they are added as competitors.
