Use this file for hook and format ideas only. Never reuse a competitor's wording, visuals or material.

## The big picture

- Engagement is tiny across the board. The best competitor post in the period got 44 likes and 4 comments (a RepCard Reel; RepCard has about 4,200 Instagram followers). Treat everything below as direction, not proof.
- Every tracked competitor talks to US roofing and home-service teams. Nobody talks to Nordic or UK field marketing, door-to-door, retail promotion or telemarketing companies. That audience is open.
- SalesScreen, Plecto and Pointagram posted almost nothing on these channels in the period. Beest is on LinkedIn only and is not tracked.

## Patterns worth using, adapted to AKITA

1. **Number plus a time box.** "6 objections in 60 seconds", "7 years of leading sales teams in 60 seconds". It promises a fast payoff. For AKITA, aim it at owners and leaders and build it on real AKITA features, never invented statistics.
2. **"Two kinds of ..." contrast.** A simple split the buyer recognises in themselves. For example, the manager who knows today's sales at 4 pm against the one who finds out on Monday.
3. **Founder story with a time span.** RepCard's second-best format. Mike has a stronger version: 20 years running field marketing teams. Use only details Mike has supplied in writing. If none are on file, skip it.
4. **Teaching beats pitching.** On the same account in the same month, RepCard's teaching Reels got 25 to 44 likes and its product-pitch Reels ("Comment DEMO") got 3 to 9. This matches the PLAYBOOK: problem first, AKITA as the fix near the end.
5. **A scene told in numbers.** Knockio: "300 doors. 4 hours. 2 real conversations." It's the same style as our approved "It's 4 pm." hook. Engagement was low on Facebook, so test it rather than rely on it.
6. **Pain list plus "which one sounds familiar?"** Knockio's list of everyday gaps drew comments and shares above its usual level. It works as a carousel or a short image post.

## What not to copy

- Trade-show and booth posts. Most of SalesRabbit's and SPOTIO's feed is this, and it means nothing to Nordic buyers.
- Feature dumps with checkmark lists and jargon such as "AI-powered Next Best Action recommendations".
- "Comment DEMO" product Reels as the main format.
- Rep-mindset and podcast clips (RepCard's Rookie on Fire). Mike has ruled out rep-life content.

## Watch out: our main message is crowded

The PLAYBOOK's main message is "One system instead of five". RepCard ("run your field sales team from one platform instead of five"), Knockio ("Five apps to run a field sales business? This owner chose one", "One app. One bill.") and SalesRabbit (Roof Rabbit "in one place") say nearly the same thing. Keep the message, because Mike chose it, but make it impossible to copy: name the specific things AKITA replaces for a field team, in the reader's own words (the Excel sheet, the WhatsApp group, the paper contract, the separate competition app), and only things AKITA really does.
